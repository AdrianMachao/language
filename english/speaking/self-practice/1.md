People in Japan are generally more inclined to follow rules and regulations. There, you don't need to invest excessive effort in managing interpersonal relationships.

However, in China, having good connections with influential people is often crucial for success. Sometimes, these relationships can be more important than one's skills or experience.

Additionally, Japanese people tend to maintain clear personal boundaries. They focus on their own matters and consciously avoid disturbing others.

In contrast, in China, it's not uncommon for people to call you even at midnight over non-urgent matters. They often don't consider this a disturbance and take it for granted.

As for me, I'm not adept at managing complex relationships and prefer environments where rules are followed. I also appreciate the Japanese work ethic, which emphasizes not bothering others.
