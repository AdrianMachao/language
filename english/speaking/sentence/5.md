It's not that I don't like the car, but it's too expensive.

结果却发现，用来表达一种出乎意料、令人失望或事与愿违的结果

# what would you do if you were me?
如果xxxx你怎么办，要接虚拟语气

take care of their financial problems: 解决他们的财务问题

# what if xxx
如果没有xxx，接一般现在时

What if they don’t approve of our earning some money in our spare time?
