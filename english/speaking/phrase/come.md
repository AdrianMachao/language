# come
## come to
这个短语强调过程的结果，即“最终达到…”
总计，达到
The bill comes to $150.
突然想到
It suddently come to me that I had left my keys inside.
涉及，谈到
When it comes to cooking, I am a complete beginner.
## come into
进入
Please come into the office and have a seat
开始进入/开始具有（某种状态）
The new law will come into force next month.
继承/获得遗产
She came into a large inheritance from her aunt.
开始存在/开始被使用
The phrase "OK" come into use in the 19th century.

# go
## go away
走开
Just go away and leave me alone
让某物消失

# run
## run away
逃离危险
The rabbit run away when it saw the fox.
逃避问题
You can't just run away from your problems.

# get
## get up
起身、起立
Please get up and give your seat to the elderly
## get down
下来、蹲下、趴下
Please get down when hearing the shot.

## get out
get out of: 从里面出去
we need get out of the house
## get away
get away from: 强调“从某处/某事离开或摆脱”
It‘s hard to get away from work emails.

get away with: 做了（错事）而侥幸成功/未受惩罚
He can't get away with cheating.


# more than
修饰形容词/动词，表示 “非常、极其”，情感强烈。
You're more than welcome
“不仅仅…”、“…都不足以形容”
The scenery was more than beautiful; it was breathtaking

extend my sincere thanks to sb for sth
I would like to extend my sincere thanks to the entir team for their outstanding work in designing this website.
